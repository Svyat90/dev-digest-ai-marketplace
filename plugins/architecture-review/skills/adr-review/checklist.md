# ADR checklist

- [ ] Context: what problem is being solved and why now
- [ ] At least two alternatives considered, each with the reason it was rejected
- [ ] Consequences: what becomes easier, what becomes harder
- [ ] Impact on security, data and privacy
- [ ] Impact on cost (infrastructure, tokens, maintenance)
- [ ] How to reverse the decision if it does not work out
- [ ] Owner and review date
